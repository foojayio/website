# Neutrality, sponsorship and the About page

## Problems
- The site says "vendor-neutral" next to a Gold sponsor tier, a logo wall and "Sponsored Content" cards. Nothing explains how editorial and sponsorship are kept apart. A skeptical reader asks that first.
- `/what-is-foojay/` never says who runs the site, only links to "Meet the Team" and "Advisory Board".
- It has an empty "Join the discussion" heading, awkward headings ("Why we're here?", "Where the name comes from?"), and a name-origin section longer than the contribution section.
- Contribution paths are buttons only, with no description of what is wanted or how review works.

## Plan
1. **Needs a decision from Frank:** who is the accountable publisher to name on the page (Azul, the Advisory Board, both)?
2. Write a short "How Foojay is funded and edited" page (about 200 words): who pays, what sponsors do and do not get, how articles are chosen, how sponsored content is labelled. Link it from the homepage hero area, the footer and the "Sponsored Content" label.
3. Fix `content/pages/what-is-foojay.md`: remove the empty heading, fix the headings, name the operators near the top, shrink the name-origin section, and expand the contribution section with a link to `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
4. Make the "Sponsored Content" label link to the funding page.
5. Update `/ai-instructions/` so its description of neutrality matches the new page.

## Done when
- A reader can find "who runs this, who pays, who decides" in one click from the homepage.
- No empty headings remain on About.
