# Contributor experience

## Problems
- Contribution appears only as buttons ("Write for Foojay", "Join our Slack", "Edit this page on GitHub").
- Nothing says what is wanted, what gets rejected or how long review takes.
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` exists but isn't surfaced where the buttons are.

## Plan
1. Add a `/write-for-foojay/` section (or expand the existing "How to submit your next article" page) with: topics wanted, topics declined, review process and typical time, how scheduling works (a future `date:`, no time), and a link to `template/post.md`.
2. Link it from the "Write for Foojay" button, the footer and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
3. Keep the author's steps minimal: do not add frontmatter keys or flags (the top goal in `AGENTS.md`).
4. Make sure `template/README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` agree with the page.

## Done when
- A first-time contributor can go from the homepage to a pull request without reading anything outside the site.
