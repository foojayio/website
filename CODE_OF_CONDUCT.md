# Code of Conduct

The full, up-to-date version of this page lives on the site itself:

**→ [Code of Conduct](https://foojay.io/code-of-conduct/)**

## In short

Foojay only works because Java developers, JDK engineers and competing
vendors are willing to write and read in the same place. Whether you're
opening a pull request here, leaving a comment on an article, or writing a
post:

- Stay vendor-neutral in spirit — argue the technical point, not the company
  or person behind it.
- Assume good faith. Blunt technical feedback and code review are normal and
  welcome; personal attacks and harassment are not.
- Credit your sources, and don't submit plagiarised or fabricated content.
- Be friendly and respectful: welcome newcomers, listen to other opinions, and
  keep feedback constructive and kind.
- Keep it on topic — no spam, no pure advertising.

This applies to this repository, comments on foojay.io, and any foojay-run
podcast or event.

To report a problem, email **[hello@foojay.io](mailto:hello@foojay.io)** or
contact any member of the [editorial team](https://foojay.io/meet-the-team/)
directly. See the [full page](https://foojay.io/code-of-conduct/) for how
reports are handled.
