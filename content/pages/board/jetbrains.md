JetBrains is a global software vendor that creates professional software development tools and advanced collaboration solutions that are trusted by more than 10 million users. Since 2000, JetBrains has built a significant catalog of products, including IntelliJ IDEA, ReSharper, PhpStorm, PyCharm, Rider, Space, and more. IntelliJ IDEA is the leading IDE for Java and Kotlin development. It helps you produce high-quality code more efficiently, provides everything you need out of the box, and takes care of routine tasks so you can stay focused on the bigger picture. IntelliJ IDEA is used and loved by millions of developers from all around the world.
