Once a month, [Artur Skowronski](/today/author/artur-skowronski/) hands the first
edition of [JVM Weekly](https://www.jvm-weekly.com/) over to Foojay: a roundup of
what the community published here, led by one article he reads closely. This page
collects those editions. Each one links straight back to Artur's newsletter, and
to the Foojay articles it covered.
