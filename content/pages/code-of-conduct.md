Foojay exists because Java developers, JDK engineers and vendors who
otherwise compete with each other are willing to write and read in the same
place. That only works if the place is one people from any background,
company or experience level want to be in.

This page says what we expect from anyone who submits an article, opens a
pull request, leaves a comment, or takes part in a Foojay podcast or event —
and what happens if that expectation isn't met.

## What we expect

- **Stay vendor-neutral in spirit.** Foojay is not a platform for any single
  company. Disagree with a technical choice, a benchmark or another vendor's
  approach if you have grounds to — but argue the technical point, not the
  company or the person behind it.
- **Assume good faith, and extend the same courtesy you'd want.** Disagreement,
  code review and blunt technical feedback are normal here and welcome. Personal
  attacks, insults, and dismissing someone because of their experience level,
  employer, nationality, gender, or any other personal characteristic are not.
- **No harassment.** This includes unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate
  intimidation, stalking, sustained disruption of discussions, and publishing
  others' private information without consent.
- **Credit sources and tell the truth about content.** Don't submit plagiarised
  writing, fabricated benchmarks, or an article whose main purpose is
  advertising rather than teaching something.
- **Keep it on topic.** Comments and articles should engage with the Java/JVM/Developer
  subject at hand; unrelated promotion, spam, and vote/traffic manipulation
  aren't welcome.

## How we communicate

- **Be friendly and welcoming.** Greet newcomers, thank people for their
  contributions, and remember that many of us write in a second language and
  are not all equally experienced.
- **Respect other opinions.** Java has many valid ways to do the same thing.
  Listen to understand before replying, and accept that reasonable people can
  disagree and leave it there.
- **Be constructive.** Say what could be better and why, and offer a suggestion
  rather than only a criticism. Critique the work, not the person.
- **Keep your tone kind and clear.** Written text loses nuance, so avoid
  sarcasm and put-downs, and ask a question before assuming bad intent.
- **Take disagreement calmly.** If a discussion heats up, step away, or take it
  to a private message; if it doesn't resolve, ask the editorial team to help.

## Where this applies

Everywhere Foojay is the venue: articles and their frontmatter, pull
requests and issues on
[github.com/foojayio/website](https://github.com/foojayio/website), comments
on articles, and Foojay-run podcast recordings or events. It also covers
private communication (email, direct messages) between participants when it
concerns Foojay.

## Enforcement

The editorial team reviews reported issues and decides on a response
proportionate to what happened — from a note asking someone to rephrase, through
edits or removal of the offending content, to no longer accepting
contributions from someone who repeats the behaviour after being asked to
stop. Deliberate harassment or targeting of an individual can result in an
immediate and permanent block, without a warning first.

This is separate from, and doesn't replace, the content and account
provisions in the [Terms of Use](/terms-of-use/) — for example, the terms'
restrictions on scraping, spam, or infringing content still apply regardless
of this page.

## Reporting a problem

If something you've read, received, or seen happen on foojay.io doesn't sit
right, tell us. You don't need to be the target of it yourself, and you don't
need to have a polished case, a specific page and what happened is enough.

- Email **[hello@foojay.io](mailto:hello@foojay.io)**
- Or, for anything you'd rather not put in a public issue, contact any member
  of the [editorial team](/meet-the-team/) directly.

We'll acknowledge your report, look into it, and let you know what we did. We won't share who reported it without asking first.

This page is also kept in the website repository as
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/foojayio/website/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md),
where it is shown to anyone opening a pull request.

*This Code of Conduct draws on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), adapted for a
publishing community rather than a software project.*
