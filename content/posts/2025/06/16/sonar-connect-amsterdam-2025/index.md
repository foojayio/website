In the age of AI, ensuring code quality and code security is more critical than ever. Are you using the best methodologies to introduce GenAI in your company? do you know the risks? have you heard about AI MCP and the benefits ? what about using AI in the full SDLC? do you know the latest features of SonarQube regarding AI and SCA(dependencies vulnerabilities)?

In partnership with AWS, Sonar is inviting you to an event at the Amazon office in Amsterdam, where you will learn the answers for those questions from the hand of experts.

**09:20:** AI in Open Source \& Proprietary code: A risks for Modern Software Development?  
**10:00:** Security \& Quality by Design: Controlling Risks, Reducing costs  
**10:30:** Best Practice to Leverage AI Code Assistants  
**11:05** Break  
**11:20:** Deep Dive Sonar 2025 \& Roadmap

**12:20:** Optimizing Software costs: Why using the AWS Marketplace?

Join us at the Amazon office to:

* Discuss with Sonar \& panelists the modern challenges brought by AI and third-party and open source software

* Meet the Benelux Sonar team!
