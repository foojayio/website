**There is a lot going on in today's technological world, but the most intense and vibrant area is undoubtedly the field of artificial intelligence. After brainstorming, our Java Champion Education group agreed to create a newsletter with a 14 days cadence. In our newsletters, we will include a collection of recently read interesting resources in the field of artificial intelligence with a short description.**

Although the stream of published articles is very large and the ability to read them all is an illusion, the published selection, such as the below is implicitly narrowed. The purpose of the AI Newsletter is to help everyone stay informed and expand their knowledge in areas related to artificial intelligence.

Each record, as shown below, will hold a simple format, as following:

*article: \<title, link source\>* *date: \<YYYY-MM\>
authors: \<authors\>
desc.:\<text\>*

**L**et's start the first iteration with a batch of interesting reads:

### **article** : [GitHub CEO: manual coding remains key despite AI boom](https://www.techinasia.com/news/github-ceo-manual-coding-remains-key-despite-ai-boom "GitHub CEO: manual coding remains key despite AI boom")

* **authors**: The Times of India
* **date**: 2025-06
* **desc.:** The articles describes the importance of manual coding abilities and deep platform understanding. quote: "The worst alternative is trying to figure out how to provide feedback or prompt to describe in natural language what I already know how to do in programming language" Dohmke. Relation to VibeCoding

### **article** : [Expert Generalists](http://https://martinfowler.com/articles/expert-generalist.html#ExpertGeneralistsStillNeedSpecialists "Expert Generalists")

* **authors**: Unmesh Joshi, Gitanjali Venkatraman, Martin Fowler
* **date**: 2025-06
* **desc.:** Provides an overview of the importance of "Expert Generalist" people for problem solving during complex application development. The article outlines several scenarios of how "Expert Generalist" approaches challenges compared to specialists. The article discusses the use of LLM technologies.

### **article** : [Emerging Patterns in Building GenAI Products](https://martinfowler.com/articles/gen-ai-patterns/ "Emerging Patterns in Building GenAI Products")

* **authors**: Bharani Subramaniam, Martin Fowler
* **date**: 2025-02
* **desc.:** The article attempts to structure known ways of using LLM into patterns. It discusses their advantages and disadvantages. The article offers a closer look at their use.

* **authors**: Dave Rahardja
* **date**: 2025-06
* **desc.:** The article describes a personal perspective on how the use of AI agents negatively affects the desired project goals. It discusses the usefulness and contribution of developer skills, including the impact for future development.

* **authors**: Martin Fowler
* **date**: 2025-06
* **desc.:** This article points out the non-deterministic nature of the results generated by LLM.
