Developers deserve better. That belief is the foundation of Java District, the developer-first career platform for the Java and JVM community. We build it with developers and for developers. Their direct input shapes what we build and where we're heading.

On Java District you take control of your career. Your profile is free and you choose when you're visible. No spam, just meaningful matches with tech teams that recognize your value. We empower you with tools to showcase your skills, share your knowledge and get on the radar of the teams that matter to you. Learn from peers, stay sharp and make your expertise work for you. Every match expands your network and opens doors for what's next. Hiring partners get fair and direct access to software engineers, and the chance to put their product and team on the tech radar.
