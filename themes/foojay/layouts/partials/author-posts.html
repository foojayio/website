{{- /* Every post an author wrote, newest first. Param: the author Page. Returns: a slice of Pages (possibly empty). The author->posts link is the mirror of sponsor-posts.html: a post credits its authors as an `authors:` list of slugs (the bundle folder names under content/authors/), and older ones may still carry a single `author:` slug -- match both and de-dupe. Sorted explicitly rather than relying on site.RegularPages' default order, because `union` appends the legacy-key matches after the list-key ones. One definition, so the author profile page, the Featured Authors block and its sidebar widget all agree on what "this author's articles" and "their latest article" mean. */ -}} {{- $slug := .File.ContentBaseName -}} {{- $posts := where site.RegularPages "Section" "posts" -}} {{- $written := where $posts "Params.authors" "intersect" (slice $slug) -}} {{- $written = $written | union (where $posts "Params.author" $slug) -}} {{- return sort $written "Date" "desc" -}}
