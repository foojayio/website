{{/* Counts one read, by POSTing the page's key to the counter in worker/views/. Renders nothing unless the page is a counted one (views-key.html) AND `[params.views] endpoint` is set, so a local build never posts a hit. Everything that could be a privacy problem is absent BY CONSTRUCTION: the request carries a key and nothing else -- no identifier, no cookie, no storage outliving the tab -- and the Worker stores a key and an integer. That is also why this is a first-party endpoint rather than a hosted analytics script, which would be blocked for a large slice of a Java-developer audience and undercount badly. The sessionStorage entry is the one piece of client state, and only so a refresh does not count twice. It dies with the tab. sendBeacon so nothing about rendering waits on the counter and a reader who leaves immediately is still counted; fetch is the fallback. */}} {{- $key := partial "views-key.html" . -}} {{- with site.Params.views.endpoint -}} {{- with $key -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
