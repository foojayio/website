{{- /* The ordered steps of the series a page belongs to, or an empty slice when it is not in one. The single definition of "what the series is": the progress bar, the prev/next cards and the step count all come from here. A series is "the pages in this folder that carry a `weight`", so the only thing an author states is the ORDER -- the one thing a layout cannot work out. Titles, links, length and which step is first or last are all derived. Sibling pages WITHOUT a weight are not part of a series: the install-java pages are alternatives, not a sequence, so they get no nav rather than a wrong one. */ -}} {{- $p := . -}} {{- $steps := slice -}} {{- if and $p.File (gt $p.Weight 0) -}} {{- $siblings := where site.RegularPages "File.Dir" $p.File.Dir -}} {{- $steps = sort (where $siblings "Weight" ">" 0) "Weight" -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $steps -}}
