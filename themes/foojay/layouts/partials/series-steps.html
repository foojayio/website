{{- /* The ordered steps of the multi-page series a page belongs to, or an empty slice when it isn't in one. The single definition of "what the series is" -- the progress bar at the top of a step, the prev/next cards at the bottom, and the step count all come from here. A series is simply "the pages in this folder that carry a `weight`", so the only thing an author states is the ORDER, which is the one thing a layout cannot work out for itself. Everything else (titles, links, how many steps there are, which one is first or last) is derived, so the 20 hand-written "<< Prev / Next >>" links these pages used to carry -- which had already drifted, two pages were missing their bottom pair -- have nothing to go out of date with. Sibling pages WITHOUT a weight are not part of a series: the five install-java pages are alternatives (Windows / macOS / Linux), not a sequence, so they get no nav rather than a wrong one. */ -}} {{- $p := . -}} {{- $steps := slice -}} {{- if and $p.File (gt $p.Weight 0) -}} {{- $siblings := where site.RegularPages "File.Dir" $p.File.Dir -}} {{- $steps = sort (where $siblings "Weight" ">" 0) "Weight" -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $steps -}}
