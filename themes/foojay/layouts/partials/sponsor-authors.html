{{- /* A sponsor's `authors:` list, normalised to one shape. Param: the sponsor Page. Returns: a slice of { slug, from, till, dated } -- `from`/`till` are "" when unset, and `dated` is true when either is set. WHY THE LIST IS MIXED. An author's posts belong to a sponsor because the author worked there, and people join and leave. Two Azul authors left on 2026-04-01, and without a date their later articles -- written for someone else -- kept appearing on Azul's page. So an entry may be either: authors: - "tim-kelly" # currently there: nothing to say - slug: "pratik-patel" till: "2026-04-01" # the day they left - slug: "someone-else" from: "2024-06-01" # arrived; earlier posts aren't theirs The bare string stays the default because it is the common case (16 of Azul's 18), and a maintainer should not have to write a two-line map to say "still here" -- that is the derive/default/ask rule in CLAUDE.md. SEMANTICS: a HALF-OPEN range, [from, till). `from` is the first day attributed, `till` is the first day NOT attributed -- i.e. the day they left, which is the date a maintainer actually knows. That choice is not arbitrary: it makes the SAME date correct for both sponsors when someone moves (old employer `till: "2026-04-01"`, new one `from: "2026-04-01"`) with no gap and no double-attribution, which an inclusive `till` cannot do without an off-by-one. Both keys are optional and independent, so `till` alone means "everything up to then" and neither means "everything". Dates are pushed through `time.Format` rather than compared as written, because YAML turns an unquoted 2026-04-01 into a date object and a quoted one into a string -- and comparing those two to each other silently fails rather than erroring. Normalising both to ISO strings also makes the comparison in sponsor-posts.html a plain lexicographic one, which is correct for ISO-8601 and needs no time arithmetic. One definition, because three things read this list: sponsor-posts.html (which posts count), and the author list plus the author COUNT on sponsor/section.html. Before this they each re-read `.Params.authors` assuming it was a list of strings. */ -}} {{- $entries := slice -}} {{- range .Params.authors -}} {{- if reflect.IsMap . -}} {{- $from := "" -}} {{- $till := "" -}} {{- with .from }}{{ $from = time.Format "2006-01-02" . }}{{ end -}} {{- with .till }}{{ $till = time.Format "2006-01-02" . }}{{ end -}} {{- $entries = $entries | append (dict "slug" (.slug | default "") "from" $from "till" $till "dated" (or $from $till | not | not)) -}} {{- else -}} {{- $entries = $entries | append (dict "slug" . "from" "" "till" "" "dated" false) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $entries -}}
