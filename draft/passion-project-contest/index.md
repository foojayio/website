## The Passion Project Contest 💖

Do you have a project that started with one of these:
- "Ugh, this annoys me..."
- "Wouldn't it be cool if…"
- "Surely this exists... it doesn't?! YET?!"
- "I could build that."
- "I could build that better/cooler/for free"

And suddenly it's past midnight, you've forgotten your tea, and you're in the flow...

**Those are our kind of projects.**

Maybe you built something for your sports club or hobby. Solved a problem that kept bothering you and accidentally automated your home. Made someone's life a little easier. Or created something wonderfully unnecessary because making it made you happy.

We'd love to hear about it. What you built and what made you care enough to build it.

Enter our free [Passion Project Contest](https://www.javadistrict.eu/passion-project-submit) by **31 December 2026**. Your project could be featured in a showcase video, and you could win **€500**.

Every entrant also gets free access to our live seminar, [Self-advocacy for developers](https://www.javadistrict.eu/signup/developer). Because your work deserves to be seen.

## Why a contest like this, you ask?

Lately, we see the passion deteriorate. There's an elephant in the room. No, not PHP. AI.

There's so much talk about how we build software now, how fast we can do it, how to review all the AI output, and what developers will do next. 

I would also want to talk about what makes us want to build something in the first place for a change.

That feeling of excitement when an idea starts working. When someone uses something you made and it helps them. When you find yourself explaining your project to someone who only asked whether you wanted coffee.

I love that about our craft. And I'd love for us to share more of it. Hence this contest. Consider it a little public display of developer affection. 💖

## So bring your passion. Your stack can come too.

Yes, we're on Foojay and I'm writing this for JavaDistrict. But no, your project doesn't have to be Java.

We're capable of loving outside our ecosystem. (Yes, very mature of us indeed.)

Bring your Python, Rust, JavaScript, C#, PHP, or whatever you worked with. We won't judge your framework, architecture, the fact you vibe coded it, or number of microservices or the fact you have 17 and kubernetes for a grocery list. In fact, we don't even look at the tech implementation.

**We want to know WHAT you made and WHY.**

What sparked the idea? Who does it help? What did you learn? What made you come back to it after a long day of already telling computers what to do?

Your project can be new or something you've been nurturing for years. Enter on your own or as a team. 

## Five projects get some well-deserved love

A jury of developers, including Java Champions, will select **five projects for a showcase video**.

Then the jury and the public will choose the number 1 winner together, both jury and public accounting for **50% of the vote**. The winning project receives a **€500 gift**.

We'd love to give all five finalists a moment to say: "I made this. And I'm proud of it."

(And we won't allow you to immediately add "but it's nothing special" or "the code is a mess.") 

You put time, thought, and probably a mildly unreasonable amount of yourself into making something exist. That deserves a little spotlight. If you enter your project, you're a winner already for having a passion 💖

## Who can enter?

You can enter if you:

- Are **18 or older**
- Build software
- Live in the **EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland**

Projects in **any language or stack** are welcome.

[Read the contest terms](https://www.javadistrict.eu/passion-project-terms) for all the details. (I've been  told "just follow your heart" is insufficient as a legal framework. Very disappointing.)

## That project you're thinking of? Yes, that one we want to hear about.

The one you light up talking about.

The one your friends have heard a suspicious amount about.

The one you keep calling "just a little side project," despite caring about it enough to fix a bug on a perfectly good Saturday.

Or perhaps hardly anyone knows it exists yet. We'd love to hear about it too. Just know that if you become a finalist, I will get enthusiastic about it in public. That's sort of the arrangement. 💖

[**Submit your project by 31 December 2026.**](https://www.javadistrict.eu/passion-project-submit)

You can also [create your developer profile](https://www.javadistrict.eu/signup/developer).

Sometimes a little nudge from someone who sees you is exactly what you need to put yourself out there. Be that someone for someone else. 💖
